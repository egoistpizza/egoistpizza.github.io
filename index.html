HybridQTN

Code on GitHub

A U-Net keeps half its parameters in the bottleneck, yet that stage only links neighbouring pixels. HybridQTN replaces it with a tensor-network block that lets every position see the whole image, and the network gets smaller and better at once.

Parameters
28.9M → 16.0M
Bottleneck alone
14.2M → 1.16M
Test Dice, Kvasir-SEG
0.800 → 0.836
Boundary error, HD95
18.5 px lower
Validation Dice 0.80 reached
epoch 32, not 56

Every one of nine seed pairs, across two GPU types and two losses, favours the hybrid. It also wins on CVC-ClinicDB, with sequence-split cross-validation and when trained only on Kvasir-SEG.

Team of four students with an academic supervisor, built during inzva's AI Projects #10. I led the methodology, designed the relative (Toeplitz) operator and built most of the training and evaluation pipeline.

I want neural networks that are smaller without seeing less. Tensor networks, built to describe entangled quantum systems, store a large operator as a chain of small tensors, and the bond dimension sets how much correlation can pass along it. Before HybridQTN I captained a high-school UAV team and wrote its real-time target detection. In summer 2026 I completed QSummer, QWorld's quantum programming school.
